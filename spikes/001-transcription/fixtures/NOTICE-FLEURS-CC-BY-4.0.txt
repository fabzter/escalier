The audio excerpts and reference transcripts in this directory are derived from
the FLEURS test split (configurations en_us and es_419) and are distributed
under the same CC BY 4.0 license.  Attribution: FLEURS, Google Research.
No endorsement by Google is implied.

Changes made for this research harness:
  - selected complete, short public test recordings (no clipping of the two
    original clips);
  - converted each selected source WAV from IEEE-float 32-bit mono 16000 Hz to
    PCM16 mono 16000 Hz with the Python 3.13 standard-library converter recorded
    in fixtures/manifest.json;
  - created two explicitly derived assemblies: a repeated assembly (two copies
    of the English clip) and a synthetic EN->ES switch concatenation (the
    English clip followed by the Spanish clip, from different recordings and
    speakers), each with a concatenated reference and marked with
    derived_assembly segments and a non-"none" conversion tool in the manifest;
    the mixed fixture's conversion record carries both source configs, rows,
    item IDs and SHA-256 values, and the fixture carries language="mixed" and
    asr_language="auto";
  - copied the human-authored FLEURS reference text from the TSV
    raw_transcription column and added a trailing newline.  Reference text was
    not generated by an ASR model.

Source member filenames, TSV rows, source SHA-256 hashes and the pinned revision
are recorded in fixtures/manifest.json and fixtures/README.md.
